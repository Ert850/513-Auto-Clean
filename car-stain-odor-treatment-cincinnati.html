How we treat it

  1. Tell us what the stain or smell is and how long it's been there. A note in your booking helps.
  2. We pre-treat, agitate and extract, so what has soaked in is pulled back out instead of pushed deeper.
  3. For smells, the source gets cleaned first. Then the Ozone Odor Reset runs with the car sealed and aired out after.
  4. You see the result before you pay.

Straight answer: how much of a stain lifts depends on what it is, how long it has set and the fabric. We never promise it all comes out, and we tell you before we start.

What it costs

  • Stain Treatment, $50 to $100: Two levels. Pick the one that matches what you are dealing with.
  • Ozone Odor Reset, $50: An ozone treatment for smells that linger, run after the source is cleaned.
  • Pet Hair Removal, $50: Seats, carpets, and every crevice it has worked its way into.
  • Full Vehicle Steam Treatment, $115 ($80 with Full Interior): Every safe surface sanitized and scrubbed with a steamer.

Prices are for a small car. Medium (SUV, truck, crossover) +$10, Large (3 row SUV, 7+ passenger van) +$25. You see your exact total, with travel and tax, before you book.

Common questions

Will the stain come out completely?

Sometimes, not always. Fresh spills usually lift well. Old, set-in stains often fade a lot but leave a shadow. We tell you which you're likely to get before we start.

Can you get rid of smoke or pet smell?

Usually a lot of it. The smell lives in the fabric, so we clean the source first, then the Ozone Odor Reset works on what lingers in the air and the foam.

Do I need a full detail for a single stain?

No. Stain treatment can go on any package, and you pick surface or deep treatment depending on how bad it is.
