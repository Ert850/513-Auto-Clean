# "How it works" drafts, for the owner to approve

**Status: drafts only. Nothing here is in the catalog yet.** The live text is the `note` field of each package in `web/lib/catalog/seed.ts`. Once a draft is approved, it goes into that field, followed by `npm run build`. Never edit `index.html` directly: it is generated.

What each draft does:

- Keeps every inclusion, every exclusion and every "does NOT" from the current text.
- Is a little shorter, with plainer sentences.
- Changes no meaning. Anywhere the meaning *might* need to change to match how the business really works, the draft leaves it alone and the spot is listed under **Reality check** at the bottom, for you to decide.

Word counts are shown as current → proposed.

## Reality check: wording that may not match the business

These are deliberately **not** changed in the drafts above, because fixing them changes the meaning. Each one is your call.

1. **Full Interior: "the extraction add-on".** No add-on has that name. The nearest is **Stain Treatment, "Moderate to major removal"** (extraction plus 250 degree steam, $100), so a customer looking for "extraction" will not find it. Suggested fix: "Deep set stains need the Stain Treatment add-on (major removal)".
2. **Showroom Ready interior: "the whole cabin steamed and sanitized".** The checklist on the same card says "Full upholstery and applicable trim steam treatment", not the whole cabin. There is also a separate $75 add-on, "Full Vehicle Steam Treatment". Which one is true? If Showroom covers upholstery and trim only, say so. If it covers the whole vehicle, say "includes the Full Vehicle Steam Treatment".
3. **Showroom Ready interior: "a ceramic coating on the trim and on the interior metal and paint".** The checklist calls it a ceramic *sealant* ("Full trim ceramic sealant coating", "Ceramic sealant protection"). On the exterior side the site treats a sealant and a coating as different products at very different prices, so the interior note should use whichever word is true.
4. **Full Exterior: "which is Showroom Ready".** The exterior Showroom Ready is *Coming soon* and cannot be booked. Suggested fix: "which is Showroom Ready (coming soon)".
5. **Express Exterior: "The paint will still feel rough afterwards".** That is true of a contaminated car but not of every car. A newer or well kept car can feel smooth after a wash. "May still feel rough" is the safer claim, but it is softer, so you decide.
6. **Interior Showroom Ready duration** (not part of the note, but nearby): the catalog comment says "6 to 8 hours, quoted at 7", while the card shows "7 hrs to 8 hrs". Worth confirming which range you want customers to plan around.

The seventh "How it works" (the exterior Showroom Ready, inside the wide coming-soon card) was not in this brief, so it has no draft.
