## 1. "How it works" spacing (all package cards)

**File:** `styles.css`, the "how it works, on a front page package card" block.

**Symptoms:**
- The toggle sat 31 px under the last checklist item, far more than the 6 px between items.
- Opened, the panel sat flush on the Book button.
- The panel padding was tight.

**Root cause:** `.svc-card` is a flex column, and flex items' margins do not collapse.
- The list's `margin-bottom: 1.05rem` and the toggle's `margin-top: .9rem` added up to 31 px.
- The toggle had no bottom margin. The Book button's `margin-top: auto` is 0 in the tallest card of a row, and opening a panel makes that card the tallest, so nothing was left between the panel and the button.

**Fix:**
- The checklist drops its bottom margin when a toggle follows it (`ul.feat:has(+ .svc-how)`).
- The toggle sits .65rem (12 px including its tap padding) under the list and always keeps 1.1rem (18 px) above the button.
- The open panel gets .45rem above it and .8rem by .95rem of padding.

**Measured** at 1440, 820 and 375, both tabs, open and closed:
- Last item to toggle: 12 px on every card.
- Toggle or panel to button: at least 18 px everywhere. It is more only where a card stretches to match a taller neighbor in its row, so the buttons in a row stay level.

**Test:** on each tab, open each card's "How it works". The panel must never touch the Book button. The toggle should read as part of the checklist above it.

## 3. Paint correction / ceramic coating card

### 3a. The card's bottom-left corner

**Files:** `styles.css`, the `@media (min-width: 820px)` block (`.svc-card.wide`), plus a phone-only rule next to `.swide-main`.

**What was checked:**
- All four corner radii compute to 24 px.
- Nothing has a shadow or a transform.
- No visible child extends past the card's edge.

So the corner is not clipped or mis-rounded. The odd look comes from layout:
- **Desktop and tablet:** the card grid used `align-items: start`. The left column (`.swide-main`) ended wherever its text did, so the 22rem Register button floated above the card's bottom edge. That left a ragged empty block in the bottom-left corner, with the toggle jammed against the button. Fix: `align-items: stretch`, so the button's `margin-top: auto` pins it to the bottom, level with the tier box. The tier box keeps `align-self: start`, so it does not grow an empty tail when the left panel is opened.
- **Phone:** the tier box came directly after the Register button with **0 px** between them. The grid gap that separates them on wider screens doesn't exist when the card stacks. Fix: `margin-top: 1.4rem` on the tier box, below 820 px only.

**Test:**
- At 1440 and 820 on the Exterior tab, the Register interest button sits at the bottom of the card, level with the bottom of the grey tier box.
- At 375, there is clear space between the Register interest button and the tier box.

### 3c. Tier list alignment

**Files:** `web/scripts/render-site.mjs` (`correctionBlock`) and `styles.css` (the `.svc-corr .corr-tiers` rules).

**Root cause:** the tier `<ul>` carried the `feat` class as well as `corr-tiers`. The package-checklist rule `.svc-card ul.feat li { display: flex }` (specificity 0,2,2) outranked the intended `.svc-corr .corr-tiers li { display: block }` (0,2,1). Each tier became three flex columns (name, price, description) that squeezed each other. As a result:
- Names wrapped to 2 to 4 lines.
- Prices sat at different x positions.
- The hours landed mid-line on some tiers and alone on others, because they were joined to the description with a middle dot.

**Fix:**
- The list is now `ul.corr-tiers`, without `feat`.
- Each tier is a two-column grid: the name on the left, and the price right-aligned in its own column with tabular figures.
- Under the name are two lines, always in the same order: what the tier achieves, then "About X hrs of work".
- Tiers have equal padding and a hairline between them.

**Measured:** all four prices end at the same x (1202, 735 and 311 px at the three widths).

**Test:** on the Exterior tab, the four prices form a straight right-aligned column, and every tier shows its description on line 1 and its hours on line 2.

## 4. Offers banner

**Files:** `web/scripts/render-site.mjs` (`servicesHtml`) and `styles.css` (`.svc-note`, `.svc-offers`).

**Root cause of the icon on its own row:** `.svc-note` was `display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap`. The text's flex-basis is its full single-line width, which is wider than the banner, so it always wrapped onto a new line under the icon.

**Fix:**
- `nowrap`, with the text in a `flex: 1; min-width: 0` block.
- The icon is aligned to the first line of text.
- The two offers are now two paragraphs, divided by a dashed rule in the banner's own dashed style.
- Same colors, border and type as before.

**Test:**
- At all three widths, the (i) icon sits beside the first line.
- "Booking interior and exterior together?..." and "Have multiple vehicles?..." read as two separate lines, with a faint dashed divider between them.
