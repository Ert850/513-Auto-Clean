# FAQ questions for Elijah to answer in his own words (draft, November 15)

**What this is:** the FAQ program from the queue (`QUEUE-AFTER-FRIDAY.md`, "FAQ program"). These are the questions people type into Google about mobile detailing, plus the ones our own customers ask (`BUSINESS-CONTEXT.md` section 4).
- **About 5** go on the FAQ page.
- **The rest** feed an expanded FAQ and the service and city pages, where each answer can win a search on its own.

**Where these come from:** customer questions and common detailing searches. They are not measured search volumes yet. Before anything is built, the `niche-research` skill can rank them with real "People also ask" data.

**How to answer:**
- One to three sentences each, the way you'd text a customer. Voice notes are fine, and we'll write them up.
- Skip any you don't want on the site.
- Mark the 5 you think matter most for the FAQ page.

**What we do to every answer:**
- **No prices or numbers typed in.** Prices, times and fees are filled in from the site's settings, so they stay true when they change. Just say "it depends on the package" if you like.
- **No promises that a stain or smell comes out**, only what we do and what usually happens.

**Already on the FAQ page (6):** Do you really come to my home or work? Can you remove pet hair, stains and odors? How long does a detail take? What do you need from me on the day? How much does it cost, and how do I pay? What if I'm not happy with the result?
