# Questions for Elijah: the FAQs on the new service and area pages (November 26)

**What this is:** Imperion wants a hub of 30 to 40 pages: one for each service, add-on and area. Every page gets its own FAQ, and the answers come from you. This is the list of questions, sorted by the page they belong to.

**It replaces the old list.** `FAQ-QUESTIONS-FOR-ELIJAH.md` (November 15) had 64 questions in one list. Everything useful from it is in here, filed under the right page, with many more added. If you already answered some of the old ones, send those answers too and we'll file them.

**Where the questions come from:** what customers ask us (`BUSINESS-CONTEXT.md`), the words in our reviews, the questions on our own pages, and the questions people search for about mobile detailing. They are not ranked by search volume yet.

**How to answer:**
- Reply by number, like "12: yes, we bring our own water."
- One to three sentences each, the way you'd text a customer. Voice notes are fine. We'll write them up.
- Skip any you don't want on the site, or don't know yet.
- If a question is wrong for us, say so. That's useful too.

**What we do to every answer:**
- **No prices or numbers typed in.** Prices, times, fees and discounts are filled in from the site's settings, so they stay true when they change. "It depends on the package" is a fine answer.
- **No promises that a stain or smell comes out.** Say what you do and what usually happens.
- No customer names or details on the site.

**The key:** ✅ means the site already answers it, and says where. Answer it anyway only if you'd say it differently.

**Pages marked "Not bookable yet"** (correction and coating work) still get a page, so people can find it and register interest. Answer what you can.

## Questions only Elijah can answer for the pages

**These are facts to confirm, not FAQ questions.** The pages can't say them until you do. Reply with the letter.

- **A. Water and power.** What does the van carry: a water tank, a generator, both? Do you ever need the customer's spigot or outlet? (The FAQ page, the Terms and the Interior page say this three slightly different ways.)
- **B. Insurance.** The Terms say general liability. Is that right, and does it cover damage to the customer's car while you work on it?
- **C. Who works.** Who does what today? Is the second detailer on interior only? Which jobs do you always do yourself?
- **D. Recent jobs by area.** Which towns have you worked in over the last few months? The area pages need real local jobs (with the ZIP) before they can go live.
- **E. Reviews by area.** Which reviews can we tie to a town, with the reviewer's OK?
- **F. Northern Kentucky and Southeast Indiana ZIPs.** Which ZIPs do you serve there? Our area list has none yet for either one, so those pages can't be checked or built.
- **G. How far you go.** The Range & Travel page says you will travel up to 12 hours each way for the right job. Is that true, and should it stay?
- **H. What you turn down.** The Terms say you may decline biohazard jobs, but vomit, urine and mold are some of your biggest jobs. Where is the line?
- **I. Products.** Which brands do you use, and are any of them pet safe or fragrance free?
- **J. Vehicles you don't do.** Boats, RVs, motorcycles, exotics: yes, no, or ask first?
- **K. Garages and canopies.** Do you bring a canopy for every job or only correction work? Can you work in a public parking garage?
- **L. Headliners.** The old notes say usually not, because they can sag. Is that still your rule?
- **M. Correction and coating.** Roughly when do you expect to open Paint Correction and Protection, and the coating add-ons?
- **N. Kentucky and Indiana tax.** Do you collect their sales tax today, as the Terms say?
- **O. Gift card payments.** The gift card page says customers can pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Is that what you see on the Stripe checkout?
