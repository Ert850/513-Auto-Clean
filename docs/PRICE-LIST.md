# 513 Auto Clean: price list

Generated from the website's own catalog on November 12, 2026 (`web/scripts/price-list.mjs`). These are the prices 513autoclean.com shows and charges. Quote only these.

- **Prices are per vehicle, before Ohio or Kentucky sales tax and before travel.**
- **Vehicle size:** Small (sedan, coupe, hatchback) no extra; Medium (SUV, truck, crossover) +$10; Large (3 row SUV, 7+ passenger van) +$25.
- **Travel:** the first 10 minutes of drive time are free; after that the site works out the fee from the address before booking.
- **More than one vehicle:** 10% off each extra vehicle. **Interior and exterior together:** $25 off per vehicle.
- **Add-ons are booked with a package.** A single job on its own (one stain, a spill, odor) is quoted by text or call: see "Spot and restoration jobs".
- **Nothing is charged up front;** customers pay when the detail is finished.

## Spot and restoration jobs

One-off jobs (a single stain, a spill, vomit, pet urine, a smell that won't go) start at **$100**, and some need a photo or a call first. Text a photo to (513) 279-2915. (The $100 minimum is from the team brief, 7.1; the website doesn't book these on their own yet.)
With any interior package they are booked online as add-ons:

| Problem | Treated with | Add-on price |
|---|---|---|
| Vomit, food or drink spills | Stain treatment, deep | $100 |
| Pet urine or accidents | Stain treatment, deep, plus Ozone Odor Reset for the smell | $100 + $50 |
| Single stains on seats or carpet | Stain treatment, surface or deep | $50 to $100 |
| Smoke, mildew or lingering odors | Ozone Odor Reset | $50 |
| Pet hair | Pet Hair Removal | $50 |
| Germs and allergens (sanitizing) | Full Vehicle Steam Treatment | $115 ($80 with Full Interior) |
| Under the front seats | Seat Removal | $100 |

Say "treat", "lift" or "reduce", never "remove" or "eliminate": results depend on the fabric and how long the stain has set.
