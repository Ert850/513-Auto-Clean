1. Who we are

513 Auto Clean is a mobile car detailing business operating in Greater Cincinnati, Ohio, along with Northern Kentucky and Southeast Indiana. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean 513 Auto Clean. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use 513autoclean.com or contact us about a detail.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the quote request form on our website, or when you call, text, or email us, you may provide:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your vehicle (make and model)
  • Your city or ZIP code
  • The service you are interested in, and anything you tell us about your vehicle’s condition or your scheduling preferences

Only your name and phone number are required. Everything else is optional and just helps us give you an accurate quote.

Information collected automatically

Our website is a simple static site. We do not run analytics software, advertising pixels, or session-tracking tools on it. However, like nearly all websites, our web host records standard server logs (including IP addresses and browser type) for security and reliability, and we load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. See Cookies and analytics below.

3. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your quote request and give you pricing
  • Schedule your detail and confirm the time and location
  • Contact you about your appointment, including if we are running late or need to reschedule
  • Follow up after service to make sure you are happy with the work
  • Keep basic business records

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number, we may contact you by text message about your quote or your appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (513) 279-2915.

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except with the messaging service providers that help us deliver the messages themselves.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is never a condition of purchasing anything from us.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

  • Web3Forms — processes our website quote form and delivers it to our email inbox.
  • Google — we use Gmail for email, and our website loads fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Our web host — serves the website and keeps standard server logs.

We may also disclose information if we are required to by law, or if it is necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property.

6. Cookies and analytics

As of the effective date above, this website does not set any cookies of its own, and we do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which are served from Google’s servers. This means Google receives your IP address and browser information when a page loads, even though we do not set a tracking cookie.

If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any non-essential tracking is loaded.

7. How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and customer records for as long as needed to serve you and to keep reasonable business and tax records, generally up to seven years for records tied to a completed, paid job. If you asked for a quote but never booked, you can ask us to delete your information at any time and we will.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you, by phone, text, or email

Just email 513autoclean@gmail.com or call (513) 279-2915 and we will take care of it. We will not charge you or treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy law. We honor these requests regardless of whether a specific law applies to you.

9. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to customer information to the people who need it to do the work. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we do not collect more than we need, which is the best protection there is.

10. Children’s privacy

Our services are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, for example when we launch online booking and start processing payments, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top. Significant changes will be made clear on the website.
