Why Delhi & Price Hill drivers book us

Delhi Township runs from the ridge down to the Ohio River, and Price Hill sits on the hill above it, so a lot of driving here is up and down: Delhi Road past Mount St. Joseph University, Rapid Run Road, Glenway Avenue, and River Road, where U.S. 50 carries heavy truck traffic along the water. Cars that do those trips every day collect grit in the floor mats and dust in the vents faster than most.

We come to you instead. Park in your driveway in Delhi Hills or Delshire, on your street in East or West Price Hill, or at work, and we detail the car where it sits while you get on with your day. You see your exact price, travel and tax included, before you book, and you pay on site once the detail is done.

  • Full Interior and Full Interior + Steam for family cars, SUVs and minivans
  • Stain, spill and odor treatment for seats and carpet
  • Exterior washes, decontamination and protection in the warmer months
  • Detailing at your home, or at your workplace while you work
