Driveway, street or work lot

If you have a driveway, that's usually where we set up. Street parking can work too, as long as there's a safe, legal spot right beside the car with room to open every door. If there's nowhere good to work near you, tick "I do not have a good location for a detail near me" when you book and we'll meet at a nearby lot. We bring what we need, and an outdoor outlet helps if you have one. You don't have to be home, and the car can be at work: tell us where it's parked.

For most people it's the inside that needs the work: car seats, dogs, work boots and months of commuting. That's what a Full Interior is for. If there's a stain or a smell you're worried about, mention it when you book, and we'll tell you what to expect before we start.

Common questions

I only have street parking. Can you still do it?

Often, yes, if there's a safe, legal spot right beside your car. If there isn't, tick "I do not have a good location for a detail near me" when you book and we'll meet at a nearby lot.

Can you do my car while I'm at work?

Yes, if your lot allows it. Add a note about where the car is parked and how we'll get the keys.

How is travel priced to Delhi or Price Hill?

By the drive to your address. The booking form works it out and puts it in your total, with tax, before you book. Nothing is added after.
