1. Who we are

513 Auto Clean is a mobile detailing business based in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving Greater Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky and Southeast Indiana. Throughout this page, "we" and "us" mean 513 Auto Clean, and "you" means the person booking the detail.

By booking, you confirm that you are at least 18, that you are the owner of the vehicle or have the owner's permission to have it detailed, and that the card you give us is yours to use.

2. Booking and confirmation

When you book online you pick the time that suits you. We confirm it by hand, usually within a few hours, by text or email, so a booking is not final until you hear from us. Once in a while a time needs shifting and we will get in touch and sort out another one with you. Nothing is charged before that.

Sometimes we cannot offer a specific time and you tell us instead which days and times of day suit you. That is a request, not a confirmed booking. No time is held and nothing is charged until we come back to you with a time and you accept it.

We ask for a card when you book. Nothing is charged then unless you choose to pay in full. It is there so that a last-minute cancellation is not free for the person making it. See cancellation.

3. Your price, and when it can change

The price you see when you book covers the package and add-ons you picked, for the size of vehicle you told us, plus travel and Ohio sales tax. That is the price. It is what we charge.

Travel

The first 10 minutes of drive time are free. After that we charge for the drive out to you rather than a flat call-out fee, worked out from your address and the time you picked. You see the figure before you confirm and it is locked in there. If the traffic is worse on the day, that is our problem, not yours.

If the vehicle needs more than the package covers

Every package buys a set amount of time and a set list of work. Now and then a vehicle turns up needing a lot more than that: heavy pet hair, mold, biological mess, staining right through the interior, or damage nobody mentioned.

That does not change your price. We work to the package you booked, for the time it buys, and we get it as far as we can. A Basic Interior at $125 is a Basic Interior at $125 whatever we find when we open the door. What changes is how much of the problem we can reach in that time, and we will tell you honestly where we expect to get to before we start.

If you would rather we went further than the booked time allows, that is the one conversation where a different number comes up:

  • We tell you what we found, what it would take to finish it properly, and what that would cost.
  • You decide. Go ahead at the new figure, stay with what you booked, or book the rest for another day.
  • Say no and nothing changes: you get the detail you booked at the price you booked, and there is nothing extra to pay.

We will never start work and then hand you a bigger bill. No price moves without you agreeing to it first, in words, before the work it pays for.

A few add-ons carry an asterisk on the booking page. Those are quoted from a starting figure because the work genuinely scales with how much there is of it, and the same rule applies: you hear the number and agree to it before we start.

4. Payment

Normally you pay when the work is done. On the day you can pay by cash, check, card, tap to pay, Venmo, Apple Pay, Cash App or Zelle. You can also pay in full online when you book, or ask us to put it on the card we already have on file.

You can pay in full up front instead, which gets you a discount. That is always your choice and we will never ask for it.

If your booking is a request rather than a confirmed time, we do not offer pay in full at all. We are not going to sit on your money for a time nobody has agreed to yet.

When you book you give us a card and tick a box. That box authorizes one thing: if you cancel or move the booking late, we may charge the fee in section 5 to that card. It is not permission to charge you for the detail itself. We record the wording you agreed to and the version of these terms alongside your booking.

The detail itself you pay for however you prefer: on the day by cash, check, card, tap to pay, Venmo, Apple Pay, Cash App or Zelle, or by asking us to put it on the card we already hold.

If a payment fails we will tell you and try again, or take one of the other methods above. If a balance is still unpaid 14 days after the detail we may add a late payment charge of $25 and, after 30 days, pass it to a collection agency. We would much rather sort it out with you directly, so tell us if something has gone wrong.

Prices are in US dollars and include sales tax at the rate for the state and county where the work is performed. Vehicle washing, waxing and detailing are taxable services in Ohio, and we collect the applicable rate in Kentucky and Indiana when we work there.

5. Cancellation, rescheduling and changes

Moving a booking always beats cancelling it. The amount at stake is the same either way, but if you reschedule, every cent of it goes onto your new date instead of being kept. With more than 72 hours notice there is nothing to pay at all.

Notice givenIf you cancelIf you reschedule
72 hours or more before No charge Free, nothing to pay
24 to 72 hours before 50% of the booking, kept 50% taken now, all of it credited to the new date
Less than 24 hours before The full booking, kept Paid in full now, credited for 30 days, plus a flat 10% late move fee

Why it works this way

A booked slot is a slot we turned other people away for. Inside 72 hours the day is half committed and hard to refill; inside 24 hours it is gone entirely, because everyone who wanted that time has booked with someone else.

So the charge is the same whether you cancel or move, and the only difference is where the money ends up. Cancel and we keep it. Reschedule and it sits against your new date, and you have lost nothing. We would far rather detail your car next week than keep your money this week.

It is also why we take card details when you book. We are not charging you up front. We just need a late change to cost the person making it something, rather than costing everyone else the slot.

Changing what is included

Just tell us what you want changed. Adding or dropping services is free any time up to 72 hours before your slot. Inside 72 hours it depends on whether the change needs time that was not already yours.

  • Keep your slot and add to it. Say you booked 10am to 2pm and now want the outside done too, so it runs 8am to 2pm. The four hours you had were always yours. Only the new 8am to 10am was time somebody else could have taken, so the 20% short notice rate applies to the added work only, not to your whole booking.
  • Move onto different time. Say that same 10am to 2pm becomes 8am to 12pm. You have handed back 12pm to 2pm, which we cannot fill at this notice, and taken 8am to 10am, which we could have sold. That is a new booking rather than a bigger one, so the 20% applies to the whole thing.
  • Drop services or finish earlier. Always free. Handing time back helps us.

The short version: keep every minute you already have and you only ever pay the rate on what you add. Ask us and we will work out the number for you before you agree to anything.

Using your credit

Credit from a reschedule is good for 30 days from the appointment you moved, and comes straight off the price of the new booking. If the new detail costs more, you pay the difference. If it costs less, we hold the rest for you for the remainder of the 30 days.

Moving a booking inside 24 hours

You can still do it, and your money still follows you. There is one extra charge: a flat 10% late move fee, charged each time you move a booking at that notice. It stays 10% every time. Moving twice means two separate 10% charges, not 10% and then 20%.

We are a small operation and a day only holds so many jobs. One booking bouncing around on the morning can knock out two or three other people, and if moving it costs nothing there is no reason not to keep doing it. Ten percent is small enough to shrug at once and big enough to make you check your calendar before the third time.

We are reasonable about this

We do not have to enforce any of the above, and often we do not. We are not going to charge you because someone in your family ended up in hospital, or your car got hit, or you came down with something. Tell us what happened and we will work it out.

We would rather keep a customer than win an argument over one booking. The point of all this is to keep the schedule steady, not to catch anyone out or make money off a bad week.

How to cancel or move a booking

Call or text (513) 279-2915, or reply to the confirmation we sent you. A text is fine and you do not need a reason.

If you want to move it rather than cancel, say so, because moving is nearly always cheaper for you. Tell us roughly when suits and we will find you something.

If we have to cancel

We will tell you the moment we know and offer you the next time we have. If we cancel, there is no charge and you get back anything you paid, however short the notice. Weather is the usual reason: rain, freezing temperatures and high wind all wreck an exterior result. Illness and van trouble happen too.

If nobody is there

If we turn up at the agreed time and cannot reach you or get to the vehicle, we will call, text and wait a while. If we still cannot start, it counts as a cancellation inside 24 hours. Catch us before we set off and we will treat it as a reschedule instead, so your money stays with you.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, the same day. Your bank usually takes 5 to 10 working days to show it. We do not keep a processing fee out of it.

If you think a charge is wrong, tell us first. We answer the same day and we fix mistakes without an argument. A dispute raised with your bank before you have given us the chance to put it right costs us a fee and weeks of paperwork, and we will treat it as a cancellation without notice for the purposes of any future booking.

6. What we need from you on the day

We work at your place, so we need a few things from you:

  • Somewhere to work. A driveway, a parking space or a garage, with enough room to open the doors and walk around the vehicle. If you do not have a good spot, tell us when you book and we will sort something out with you.
  • Access to the vehicle. Keys or an unlocked vehicle at the agreed time.
  • Water and power if you have them. We bring our own if you do not, but tell us beforehand rather than when we arrive.
  • Your things out of the car. Take out anything valuable, fragile or important. We will work around whatever is left, but we cannot be responsible for it, and a full car takes longer and cleans up worse.

7. What a detail can and cannot do

Every package on the booking page says what it does and what it does not. Broadly:

  • Detailing is cleaning and protection, not repair. Tears, burns, cracked trim, worn material, dents and rock chips are still there afterwards.
  • Stains often fade rather than vanish. How much lifts depends on what it is, what it is on, and how long it has been sitting. We will give you our honest read before we start.
  • Scratches depend on depth. Anything you can catch a fingernail in has gone through the clear coat and cannot be polished out, because correction removes clear coat and cannot add any.
  • Smells do not always clear if the source is still in the car, up in the vents, or down in the padding under the carpet.

If we can see you are not going to get the result you are hoping for, we will tell you before we take the booking or before we start.

8. Damage, and our insurance

We carry general liability insurance and we look after every car like it is ours. If we damage something, tell us before we leave or as soon as you spot it, and we will put it right or claim on our insurance.

What we cannot cover:

  • Pre-existing damage, wear or defects, including paint already failing, clear coat already peeling, or trim already cracked.
  • Things that give way during ordinary cleaning on an older or previously repaired car, like a badly fitted aftermarket part coming loose.
  • Water entering a vehicle through a failed seal, a window left open, or existing bodywork damage.
  • Items left in the vehicle.
  • Wraps, paint protection film or resprayed panels we were not told about. Mention them and we will change our products and our approach.

Beyond what our insurance pays out, our liability to you for anything arising from a detail is limited to the price you paid for it. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost time, lost income or the cost of a hire car. Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or anything else the law does not allow us to limit.

9. Our make it right guarantee

If something about the finished detail is not right, tell us within 30 days and we will come back and sort it out for free. That covers work we did, or should have done, as part of the package you booked. It does not stretch the package into work you did not book, and it does not cover dirt from driving the car since.

10. Photos and video

We ask separately, at the time of booking, whether we may photograph and film the detail, and we honor the answer. The question is not pre-ticked and answering no does not affect your price or your booking in any way.

If you say yes, we may record the work, edit it into clips, and publish them on our own social media and website to show the standard of our work. We always blur children, license plates, house numbers and any other private information before anything is published. If you change your mind afterwards, tell us and we will take it down.
