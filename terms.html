1. Who we are

513 Auto Clean is a mobile detailing business based in Cincinnati, Ohio, serving Greater Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky and Southeast Indiana. Throughout this page, "we" and "us" mean 513 Auto Clean, and "you" means the person booking the detail.

By booking, you confirm that you are the owner of the vehicle or that you have the owner's permission to have it detailed.

2. Booking and confirmation

Booking online reserves a time on our calendar. You will get a confirmation by text or email. If we cannot make the time you picked, we will contact you to arrange another before anything is charged.

Sometimes we cannot offer a specific time and you tell us instead which days and times of day suit you. That is a request, not a confirmed booking. No time is held and nothing is charged until we come back to you with a time and you accept it.

We ask for a card when you book. It is not charged at the time of booking unless you choose to pay in full, which is explained below.

3. Your price, and when it can change

The price you see when you book covers the package and add-ons you selected, for the size of vehicle you told us, plus travel and Ohio sales tax. It is not an estimate; it is the price, and it is what gets charged.

Travel

The first 10 minutes of drive time are free. Past that, travel is priced on the real measured drive to your address at the time of your appointment, not a flat call-out fee. That figure is worked out and shown to you before you confirm, and it is frozen at that point. It does not change afterwards, even if the traffic on the day turns out differently.

Adjusting the price on arrival

This is the one circumstance in which the price can change, and it matters, so it is spelled out.

Detailing is priced on the condition of the vehicle. Occasionally a vehicle needs materially more work than the package booked: heavy pet hair, biological contamination, set-in staining across the interior, mould, or damage that was not described. If that happens:

  • We tell you before any work starts, not afterwards.
  • We explain what we found, what it will take, and what it will cost.
  • You choose. You can accept the revised price, drop back to a smaller package, reschedule, or decline entirely.
  • If you decline, you pay nothing at all. No call-out fee, no travel charge, no cancellation fee. We will have driven out for nothing, and that is our risk to carry, not yours.

We will not begin work and then present a larger bill. If we have started, the price we agreed is the price you pay.

Some add-ons are marked with an asterisk on the booking page for exactly this reason. That asterisk means the quote is a starting figure and may change on inspection, and the same rule above applies: you hear the number before we begin.

4. Payment

The default is that you pay when the work is finished, using the card you gave when booking or any other method you prefer on the day. We accept cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Venmo and cash.

You may choose to pay in full at the time of booking, which earns a discount shown at checkout. Paying in full is optional and never required.

Where a booking is a request rather than a confirmed time, paying in full is not offered at all, because we will not hold your money against a time that has not been agreed.

Prices are in US dollars and include Ohio sales tax at the rate for the county where the work is performed. Vehicle washing, waxing and detailing are taxable services in Ohio.

5. Cancellation and rescheduling

Cancel or reschedule any time before we arrive, for any reason, and you owe nothing. There is no cancellation fee and no deposit to lose. If you paid in full and cancel, you get a full refund.

How to cancel or move a booking

Call or text (513) 279-2915, or reply to your confirmation message. A text is fine. We do not require a reason.

Notice we would appreciate

We are a two person business and a booked slot is a slot we turned others away for. As much notice as you can give is genuinely helpful, and 24 hours is ideal. This is a request, not a rule, and nothing happens if you cannot give it.

Refunds

If you paid in full and then cancel, we refund the full amount to the original payment method. Refunds are issued the same day and typically appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. We do not retain a processing fee.

If we have to cancel

We will tell you as soon as we know and offer the next available time. If we cancel, anything you have paid is refunded in full. We may need to reschedule for weather, because rain, freezing temperatures and high wind genuinely prevent a good exterior result, or for illness or vehicle trouble on our side.

If nobody is there

If we arrive at the agreed time and place and cannot reach you or access the vehicle, we will call and text and wait a reasonable time. If we still cannot start, we treat it as a cancellation. We do not charge for this, but we will ask you to rebook rather than absorbing repeat trips.

6. What we need from you on the day

Being mobile means working in your space, so a few things have to be true:

  • Somewhere to work. A driveway, a parking space or a garage, with enough room to open the doors and walk around the vehicle. If you do not have a good spot, tell us when you book and we will sort something out with you.
  • Access to the vehicle. Keys or an unlocked vehicle at the agreed time.
  • Water and power where possible. We bring our own where it is not, so tell us in advance rather than on the day.
  • Personal belongings removed. Please take out anything valuable, fragile or important. We will work around belongings left in the vehicle, but we cannot be responsible for them, and a cluttered interior takes longer and cleans less well.

7. What a detail can and cannot do

We describe every package on the booking page with what it does and what it does not do, and we would rather set an accurate expectation than a flattering one. In general:

  • Detailing is cleaning and protection, not repair. Tears, burns, cracked trim, worn material, dents and rock chips are still there afterwards.
  • Stains may reduce rather than disappear. How much comes out depends on what it is, what it is on, and how long it has been there. We will tell you what we think before we start.
  • Scratches depend on depth. Anything you can catch a fingernail in has gone through the clear coat and cannot be polished out, because correction removes clear coat and cannot add any.
  • Odours may not fully clear if the source is still in the vehicle, in the ventilation system, or in the padding under the carpet.

Where we can see in advance that a result will be limited, we will say so before taking the booking or before starting work.

8. Damage, and our insurance

We carry general liability insurance and we treat every vehicle as we would our own. If we damage your vehicle, tell us before we leave or as soon as you find it, and we will make it right or claim on our insurance.

Some things we cannot be responsible for, and these are the honest limits:

  • Pre-existing damage, wear or defects, including paint already failing, clear coat already peeling, or trim already cracked.
  • Faults that surface during normal cleaning on an aged or previously repaired vehicle, such as a badly fitted aftermarket part coming loose.
  • Water entering a vehicle through a failed seal, a window left open, or existing bodywork damage.
  • Items left in the vehicle.
  • Aftermarket wraps, paint protection film, or repainted panels that are not disclosed to us in advance. Tell us and we will adjust our products and methods.

9. Our make it right guarantee

If anything about the finished detail is not right, tell us within 48 hours and we will come back and fix it at no charge. This covers work we did or should have done as part of the package you booked. It does not extend the package into work you did not book, and it does not cover new dirt from normal use.

10. Photos and video

We ask separately, at the time of booking, whether we may photograph and film the detail, and we honour the answer. The question is not pre-ticked and answering no does not affect your price or your booking in any way.

If you say yes, we may record the work, edit it into clips, and publish them on our own social media and website to show the standard of our work. We always blur children, license plates, house numbers and any other private information before anything is published. If you change your mind afterwards, tell us and we will take it down.

11. Text messages

Booking confirmations, reminders and an "on our way" message are sent by text if you agree to them, and you can say no and have us email or call instead. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Marketing texts, if we ever send them, are a separate question and a separate opt in.

We never share your mobile number or your SMS consent with anyone for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for the detail.

12. When we may decline a job

We may decline or stop a job if the vehicle contains biohazardous material, if the working conditions are unsafe, if there is abusive behaviour, or if the vehicle or location turns out to be materially different from what was described. If we decline before starting, you pay nothing. If we stop partway for one of these reasons, we charge only for work genuinely completed and will explain exactly what that covers.
