In your driveway or garage

Most homes here have a driveway or a garage, and either one is a good place for us to work. In a garage we can keep going through rain or hard sun, so tick the garage box when you book. If the house sits back from the road or there's a gate, add a note about where to park and how to get in. You don't need to be home, as long as we can get into the car.

Car seats, dogs, sports gear and road trips all end up in the back seat sooner or later. Full Interior + Steam is the one we'd pick for a family car. If yours needs more than that, ask about Showroom Ready Restoration.

Common questions

Can you work at a house with a long driveway or a gate?

Yes. We need a level spot next to the car. If the house is far back from the road or gated, tell us where to park and how to get in when you book.

Can you work inside my garage?

Yes, and it's often the best spot, because rain and sun don't matter there. Leave room to open every door of the car.

Is there a travel fee to Indian Hill?

Travel is worked out from the drive to your address, and it's in your total, with tax, before you confirm. No surprise charges later.
